Erik Sundstrup
VK4AES  ·  14 March 2015

Erik was a Queensland amateur radio operator and the inventor of EasyPal — a pioneering digital image transfer application that used DRM encoding to send photographs over standard SSB audio channels.

His work brought a new dimension to amateur radio imaging, allowing operators worldwide to exchange pictures over HF, VHF and UHF with robust error correction that analogue SSTV could not match.

Erik's source code was lost in a lightning strike. He passed away on 14 March 2015 before it could be rebuilt. The amateur radio community lost both a gifted engineer and a generous spirit.

"OpenPal exists because of what Erik built and what he inspired."
